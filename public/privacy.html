Privacy Policy for Cartagena: Escape Diaries

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Cartagena: Escape Diaries ("we", "our", or "the game") is an open-source, community-focused digital board game. We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of all players.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

  • Player Names: The screen name you input when creating or joining a room is transient and only displayed to players within your active game room.
  • Game State & Chat: Game events, moves, and optional in-room chat messages are held in server memory for the duration of the match and are not permanently archived or sold.

3. Children's Privacy

The game does not collect personal data from children under the age of 13. It contains no advertisements, no tracking SDKs, and no microtransactions.

4. Third-Party Services

Cartagena: Escape Diaries does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or social media tracking pixels.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out via GitHub or project repository issues.
