Cartagena Escape Rules

🎯 The Escape Goal

First captain to get all 6 pirates (or 4 in Quick Game) through the 36-space tunnel onto the Rowboat wins immediately!

⚡ Up to 3 Actions per Turn

On your turn, you can mix and repeat up to 3 actions in any order:

🏃
Action A: Dash Forward (Costs 1 Card)

Play a card from your hand. Your pirate leaps to the nearest free space ahead matching that symbol (max 3 pirates per space). If no matching space is ahead, jump directly onto the Escape Boat!

↩️
Action B: Retreat for Cards (Costs 0 Cards)

Select a pirate in the tunnel. It steps backward to the nearest space holding 1 or 2 pirates. Draw 1 or 2 cards equal to the pirates already there! (Empty spaces & Jail are skipped).

🏁
End Turn & Card Refill

When your turn ends, draw cards until you hold 6 cards (if holding fewer than 6). If you have 6 or more, you keep them all!

🎴 The 6 Symbols (102 Cards)

🍾 Rum Bottle 🔫 Pistol 🗝️ Jail Key 💀 Skull ⚔️ Cutlass 🎩 Tricorn Hat

The tunnel has 36 spaces (6 tiles × 6 symbols). Capacity: max 3 pirates per space.
